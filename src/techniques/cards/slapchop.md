# Slapchop

Paint fast and still get a good result, especially for groups of miniatures. It combines a grisaille, drybrushing and translucent colour on top.

## Steps
- 1. **Black primer.** Let it dry completely.
- 2. **Grey drybrush** (72050 Gris Neutral or 72049 Gris Muralla) on the raised areas and edges, where light would fall. Do not cover all the black: the recesses must stay dark.
- 3. **White drybrush** (72001 Blanco Calavera), just a dusting, only on the highest points. All the light and shade is done before any colour.
- 4. **Translucent colour** zone by zone: each paint thinned (see the dilution card) so the sketch underneath shows through.
- 5. **Details** (faces, eyes, focal points) with BSL.

## Tips
- Before adding colour, check the sketch: near-white ridges and black recesses. If it does not look like that, repeat the white drybrush.
- Too much white makes the miniature chalky and the colour on top looks washed out. Better to stop short and repeat.
- If a colour hides the sketch, thin it more; if it only sits in the recesses like a wash, you went too far.
- Many painters use contrast-style paints for this. Here the same effect comes from thinning opaque paints.
