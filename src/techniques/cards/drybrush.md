# Drybrush

Quickly pick out the raised detail a miniature already has: feathers, hair, chainmail, rocks.

## How
- 1. An old stiff brush or a drybrush (B07001 Dry Brush Small). Never your good detail brush: this wears the tip out.
- 2. Unthinned paint, thick, almost a paste.
- 3. Wipe almost all of it off on a lint-free cloth or cardboard, until the brush barely marks.
- 4. Pass it lightly and quickly over the relief in several directions. Only the ridges should catch paint.
- 5. Repeat with a lighter colour on a smaller area.

## Combinations
- Slapchop sketch: 72050 or 72049, then 72001.
- Feathers or bone: 72061 Caqui, then 72098 Carne Élfica.
- Grey fur: 72049 over 72155.

## Common mistakes
- Not wiping off enough: the most common mistake. It leaves blotches and fills the recesses.
- Wiping off too much and scrubbing: a chalky dust is left.
- Pressing the brush: it also paints what should stay dark.
- Going over paint that is still wet.
- Using it on smooth surfaces: it only works where there is relief.
