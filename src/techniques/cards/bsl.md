# BSL (Base, Shadow, Light)

Painting details (faces, skin, focal points) with colours that already match, without complicated mixes.

## The roles
- **Base:** the colour of the area. It is the most opaque; apply it in one or two thin coats.
- **Shadow:** darker and slightly satin. It goes in recesses and folds.
- **Light:** lighter. It goes on what sticks out most and on edges.
- **Extra light:** the fourth and lightest tone, only on dots and edges.

## Order
- 1. Base in thin coats.
- 2. Shadow in the deep areas.
- 3. Light on the raised areas, leaving base visible between both.
- 4. Extra light on the highest points.
- 5. A glaze of the base to blend the transitions.

## Tips
- For an in-between tone, mix a colour with its neighbour in the family (50/50 or 70/30).
- Highlight with the lightest colour of the family, not with white: white dulls the colour, especially yellows and oranges.
- Only mix colours from the same family and temperature. An outsider makes the mixes muddy.
- On blacks and greys keep the lights small: if they spread, it stops reading as black.
